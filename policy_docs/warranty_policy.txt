NovaCart Product Warranty Policy

Certain products sold on NovaCart come with a limited warranty.

Warranty Coverage
- Electronics include a 12-month manufacturer warranty.
- Warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal usage.

What Is Not Covered
The warranty does not cover:
- Physical damage (drops, spills, cracks)
- Damage caused by unauthorized modifications
- Normal wear and tear
- Software issues caused by third-party apps

Warranty Claim Process
To claim warranty:
1. Contact support with your order ID and issue description
2. Provide photos or videos if requested
3. Ship the product to the authorized service center if required

Warranty Resolution
After inspection:
- The product may be repaired, replaced, or rejected if misuse is found.
