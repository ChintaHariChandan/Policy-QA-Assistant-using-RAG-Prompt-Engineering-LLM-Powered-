NovaCart Cancellation Policy

We understand that plans change. You may cancel your order under the following conditions.

When You Can Cancel
Orders can be cancelled:
- Within 12 hours of placing the order
- Before the order status changes to “Shipped”

When Cancellation Is Not Possible
Orders cannot be cancelled if:
- The order has already been shipped
- The product is custom-made or personalized

Refund After Cancellation
- Approved cancellations receive a full refund.
- Refunds are processed within 5 business days.

How to Cancel
Customers can:
- Log into their NovaCart account and click “Cancel Order”
- Or contact customer support with their order number
