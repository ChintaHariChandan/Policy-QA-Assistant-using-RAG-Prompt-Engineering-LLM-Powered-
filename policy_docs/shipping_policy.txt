NovaCart Shipping Policy

We strive to deliver your products safely and on time.

Shipping Locations
We currently ship to:
- All major cities within the country
- Selected international destinations (availability shown at checkout)

Delivery Time
- Standard shipping: 5–7 business days
- Express shipping: 2–3 business days

Delivery times may vary due to weather, holidays, or courier delays.

Shipping Charges
- Orders above ₹5,000 qualify for free standard shipping.
- Express shipping incurs additional charges calculated at checkout.

Order Tracking
After dispatch, customers receive:
- A tracking number via email
- A link to the courier tracking page

Delayed or Lost Shipments
If your package is delayed beyond 10 business days:
- Contact customer support with your order ID
NovaCart will coordinate with the courier to resolve the issue.
